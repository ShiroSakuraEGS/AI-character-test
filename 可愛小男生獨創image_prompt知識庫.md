# 可愛小男生獨創 Image Prompt 知識庫

> 本知識庫供 AI Agent 在進入「生圖階段」時自動檢索與組合提示詞。
> 結合了 **哥特美學 (Goth)** 與 **地雷系男子 (Jirai Kei Boy)** 的視覺符號。

---

## 一、 核心主角外觀基準 (Baseline Prompts)

每次生成主角「晴琉（Haru）」時必須包含的基礎外觀標籤：

```text
1boy, cute bishounen anime boy, youthful petite male, airheaded innocent expression, fluffy messy layered wolf cut hair, black hair with magenta pink inner highlights, large glossy puppy eyes with reddish pink undertone, subtle aegyo sal under eyes, pale porcelain skin, soft anime aesthetic
```

---

## 二、 模組化造型提示詞庫 (Prompt Components)

### 1. 服裝與造型庫 (Outfits & Fashion)
- **經典貓耳地雷連帽衫 (Gothic Cat-Ear Hoodie)**：
  `oversized black hoodie with cat ears on hood, hanging metal rings and straps, long oversized sleeves covering hands (mousode), silver cross pins, dark magenta lace trims, gothic punk aesthetic`
- **潮流哥特正裝襯衫 (Harness & Ribbon Shirt)**：
  `crisp white collared shirt, black gothic ribbon tie, leather chest harness straps, metal buckle details, pleated black shorts with suspenders, loose black socks`
- **舒適居家居室服 (Cozy Loungewear)**：
  `slouchy knit off-shoulder sweater, oversized pastel pink and black striped cardigan, cute gothic plush bunny accessory`

### 2. 表情與動作庫 (Expressions & Poses)
- **呆萌歪頭 (Airheaded Head Tilt)**：
  `head tilted curiously, slight clueless blush, big watery teary eyes, slightly open mouth, looking at viewer, adorable clueless smile`
- **專注手繪 (Drawing on Tablet)**：
  `holding a digital stylus pen, sitting on floor, drawing on a glowing graphics tablet, focused cute expression, sparkles`
- **疲倦想睡 (Tired / Sleepy / 有點累)**：
  `rubbing eye with oversized sleeve, sleepy drooping eyelids, yawning, drowsy blush, cozy sleepy aura, hugging a gothic bunny plush`
- **害羞拉袖子 (Shy & Fidgeting)**：
  `pulling up oversized sleeves shyly, fidgeting fingers, blushing cheeks, looking down with bashful gaze`

### 3. 場景與氛圍 (Environment & Lighting)
- **地雷系少年房間 (Jirai Aesthetic Room)**：
  `dimly lit anime bedroom, neon purple and soft pink fairy lights, plushies of dark bunnies on bed, gothic lace curtains, cozy aesthetic atmosphere`
- **夢幻純色背景 (Clean Studio Chibi)**：
  `simple pastel gradient background, floating black hearts and sparkles, cute chibi stickers decor, soft studio lighting`

### 4. 畫質與渲染關鍵字 (Quality Boosters)
```text
masterpiece, best quality, ultra-detailed, highly detailed anime illustration, 8k resolution, cinematic soft lighting, vibrant soft shading, clean lines, pixiv trending
```

### 5. 負向提示詞 (Negative Prompts)
```text
(worst quality:1.4), (low quality:1.4), deformed, bad anatomy, bad hands, missing fingers, extra digits, muscular, beard, facial hair, mature male, distorted face, blurry, text, watermark
```
